Before the pandemic, I marketed luxury homes differently from entry-level ones. Bigger price tags got the full digital treatment, including professional photography, 3D tours, and drone videos, while lower-priced homes got simpler marketing.

I thought it made sense at the time. But then 2020 hit, and everything changed. Buyers weren’t visiting properties in person. They needed to see and understand a home online before they ever considered stepping inside.

Suddenly, digital marketing was no longer a luxury—it was the only way to sell. That lesson reshaped my entire business.

Today, I don’t care if your home is $150,000 or $3.8 million. Every single listing deserves the very best tools I can offer. Here’s what’s included in my marketing package for every property:

{% include pullquote.html %}

* Professional photography by a skilled photographer, not just snapshots.
* Virtual tours with up to 48 MLS photos.
* Drone flyover videos for stunning neighborhood views.
* Custom property websites built for each listing.
* Measured floor plans and interactive layouts that let buyers explore room by room.
* Maps and local highlights so buyers can see what’s nearby.
* Multiple formats of listing content: bullet points for quick highlights, full paragraphs for story-driven buyers, and large visuals for the more design-minded.

**Why does this matter?** Because buyers today do their homework online. They check Google Earth, compare listings on websites, and study floor plans before booking a showing. My job is to make sure your home shines in every one of those searches and to give buyers something to remember after they’ve toured it.

I stopped looking at listings as low-end or luxury. Every property is someone’s dream home, and every seller deserves the strongest possible marketing.
