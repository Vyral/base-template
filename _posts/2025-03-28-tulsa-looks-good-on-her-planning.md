## Smart Financial Planning with Her Planning

Financial planning isn’t just about saving—it’s about long-term wealth and security. **Kristin Afelumo**, founder of **Her Planning**, specializes in helping women, particularly business owners and primary income earners, take control of their financial futures.

### Key Steps to Financial Success

✔ **Pay off high-interest debt** – Eliminate costly credit card balances. <br>✔ **Build an emergency fund** – Three months of expenses for dual-income households, six for single earners. <br>✔ **Get proper insurance** – Protect yourself with health, auto, and life insurance. <br>✔ **Automate savings** – Use a high-yield savings account to grow your money effortlessly.

### Proven Wealth-Building Strategies

Kristin stresses that real wealth isn’t built through social media trends but through:

* **401(k) investments** – The top long-term growth strategy.
* **Homeownership** – A solid asset for financial stability.
* **Business ownership** – A rewarding but demanding wealth path.
* **Life insurance** – A simple way to create generational wealth.

### When Should You Consult a Financial Planner?

Many hesitate to seek financial guidance, but **it’s never too early** to start. Whether you’re just beginning or managing a growing portfolio, expert advice can fast-track your goals.
