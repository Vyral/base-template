Are you trying to choose flooring that saves money while keeping your tenants satisfied? Comparing carpet and vinyl allows you to choose the best option for your rental property.

**Carpet** is a popular choice because it offers comfort and warmth that hard flooring cannot match. It is also typically more affordable to install, making it a practical option for bedrooms and living areas where comfort matters most.

Carpet is generally more affordable to install, or at least cheaper overall. That makes it a great option for bedrooms or the living room, where comfort is the priority.

**Vinyl flooring** works well in high-traffic areas like kitchens and dining spaces because it is durable and easy to clean. It is also allergy-friendly. For areas prone to spills, vinyl flooring offers durability and low maintenance while helping reduce allergens. This is a big plus for tenants who are sensitive to dust.

Ultimately, the best flooring choice depends on your priorities and how each space is used. By considering durability, comfort, and maintenance, you can select the right material for each area of your rental property.

Take a look at your budget and your target tenant to see which one fits. Both are practical choices if you use them in the right spots.
