## **How This Tulsa Nonprofit Is Fighting Food Insecurity—One Block Party at a Time**

Tulsa’s food deserts might be closer than you think—some residents are *an hour bus ride* away from fresh groceries. That’s where **Food On The Move** comes in.

Founded by Tulsa musician **Taylor Hanson** and led by **CEO Kevin Harper**, this nonprofit brings mobile food festivals directly into underserved neighborhoods. We're talking:

* 30-pound bags of fresh produce
* Meals from local food trucks
* Live DJs, dancing, and community vibes
* Health, job, and mental wellness resources

And now, they’re launching an **urban farm and aquaponics facility** right here in Tulsa—bringing innovation, education, and workforce development to the fight against food insecurity.

💬 “If we’re going to fix food insecurity, we’ve got to go all the way back to the seed.”
