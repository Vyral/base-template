Our team gets this question all the time: *“Why don’t you just post your vendor list online?”*

It’s a fair ask. We live in a time where information is just a few clicks away. So, if you’re searching for a roofer, painter, designer, or contractor , it would make sense to want a ready-made list. However, we intentionally refrain from doing that, and we want to explain why.

**We’ve built strong, trusted relationships with the most reliable vendors in Portland.** But that trust doesn’t just go one way. We also believe in making thoughtful matches based on what you need, not just giving out names and hoping one sticks.

Here’s the thing: there’s no one-size-fits-all list. A vendor who’s fantastic for a large remodel might not be the right fit for a small repair job. Some specialize in specific price ranges, neighborhoods, or project types.

That’s why we always want to hear about your goals first. Once we understand what you’re working on, we can suggest someone who’s not only skilled but also available, interested, and a good match for your project scope.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Why do we take this approach?** Our list evolves constantly. We maintain regular contact with our vendors and rely on feedback from our clients to keep those recommendations up-to-date.

If we refer someone and the experience isn’t great, we want to know. That transparency helps us keep our referral network strong and trustworthy.

Lastly, even if you’re not buying or selling a home right now, we still want to hear from you. Helping you live well in your home, whether that’s through a quick repair or a complete renovation, is still part of what we’re here to do.
