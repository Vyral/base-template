## SheBrews Coffee: Serving Second Chances in Tulsa

If you’re a coffee lover in Tulsa, there’s a place brewing more than just a great cup—**SheBrews Coffee** is transforming lives, one latte at a time. This unique coffee shop is also a **nonprofit organization** that helps formerly incarcerated women reenter society through **affordable housing, recovery services, and job training**.

In a recent interview, **Jennie Wolek of the Wolek Group, Keller Williams Realty** sat down with Daniel from SheBrews to share how this mission-driven business is making a powerful impact in our community.

### More Than Just Coffee

With locations in Tulsa’s **Kendall Whittier District** and Claremore, SheBrews employs women directly from their **transition program**, offering real-world work experience and emotional support as they rebuild their lives. Founded by **Rhonda Bear**, a woman who overcame incarceration herself, the organization has already helped **hundreds of mothers reunite with their children**—a deeply rewarding part of their mission.

### How You Can Help
